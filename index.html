私隱政策 — 今晚食咩

最後更新：2026 年 7 月 6 日

「今晚食咩」（下稱「本 App」）由 Kwan Chi Cheng（下稱「我哋」）開發及營運。我哋重視你嘅私隱，本政策解釋我哋點樣收集、使用同保護你嘅資料。

1. 我哋收集嘅資料

  • 帳戶資料：透過 Apple 或 Google 登入時，我哋會收到你嘅電郵地址同用戶 ID。
  • 雪櫃數據：你新增嘅食材名稱、數量、到期日等資料，儲存於我哋嘅伺服器（Supabase），用於同步功能。
  • 相機影像：當你使用 AI 掃描功能時，影像會傳送到我哋嘅 AI 伺服器進行食材辨識，處理完成後即時刪除，唔會永久儲存。
  • 食譜數據：你儲存嘅食譜同菜單資料。
  • 訂閱資料：訂閱狀態由 RevenueCat（第三方服務）管理，我哋只收到訂閱有效與否嘅資訊。

2. 我哋點樣使用你嘅資料

  • 提供雪櫃管理、食譜推薦同家庭共享功能
  • AI 食材辨識同食譜生成
  • 管理你嘅訂閱同服務配額
  • 改善 App 功能同用戶體驗

3. 廣告

免費版會顯示 Google AdMob 廣告。AdMob 可能會收集裝置識別碼用於個人化廣告。你可以喺裝置設定中選擇退出個人化廣告。Premium 及 Pro 訂閱用戶唔會見到廣告。

4. 資料分享

我哋唔會出售你嘅個人資料。我哋只會同以下第三方服務分享必要資料：

  • Supabase：雲端資料儲存同用戶認證
  • Alibaba Cloud (DashScope)：AI 影像辨識同食譜生成（只傳送影像同文字提示，唔包含個人資料）
  • RevenueCat：訂閱管理
  • Google AdMob：廣告投放（免費版）

5. 資料安全

所有數據傳輸使用 HTTPS 加密。我哋採用行業標準嘅安全措施保護你嘅資料。

6. 你嘅權利

  • 你可以隨時喺 App 內刪除你嘅雪櫃數據同食譜
  • 登入後可喺 App → 設定 → 帳戶 →「刪除帳戶」永久刪除帳戶同相關雲端資料
  • 有效訂閱仍需另外喺 App Store 或 Google Play 管理或取消

7. 兒童私隱

本 App 唔針對 13 歲以下兒童。我哋唔會有意收集兒童嘅個人資料。

8. 聯絡我哋

如有任何私隱問題，請聯絡：simsoncheng0502@gmail.com

Privacy Policy — Dinner?

Last updated: July 6, 2026

"Dinner?" (the "App") is developed and operated by Kwan Chi Cheng ("we", "us"). We value your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and user ID.
  • Fridge Data: Ingredient names, quantities, and expiry dates you add are stored on our servers (Supabase) for syncing.
  • Camera Images: When you use AI scanning, images are sent to our AI server for food recognition. Images are deleted immediately after processing and are not permanently stored.
  • Recipe Data: Recipes and meal plans you save.
  • Subscription Data: Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat; we only receive whether your subscription is active.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide fridge management, recipe suggestions, and family sharing features
  • AI food recognition and recipe generation
  • Manage your subscription and service quotas
  • Improve app functionality and user experience

3. Advertising

The free version displays Google AdMob ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for personalized advertising. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Premium and Pro subscribers see no ads.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share necessary data only with these third-party services:

  • Supabase: Cloud data storage and user authentication
  • Alibaba Cloud (DashScope): AI image recognition and recipe generation (only images and text prompts, no personal data)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • Google AdMob: Ad delivery (free version only)

5. Data Security

All data transmissions use HTTPS encryption. We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

  • You can delete your fridge data and recipes at any time within the app
  • After signing in, you can permanently delete your account and associated cloud data in App → Settings → Account → Delete account
  • Active subscriptions must be managed or cancelled separately in the App Store or Google Play

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy concerns, contact: simsoncheng0502@gmail.com
